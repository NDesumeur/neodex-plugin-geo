# Geo

A pixel-art geography quiz: flags, capitals and countries (names in French).

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Games

- **Flags**: find the country. **Capitals**: find a country’s capital. **Country of a capital**: the other way round.
- The whole world or one continent; ten questions with four answers.
- Missed countries come back more often.

## Controls

- **1 to 4**, or **arrows** then **Enter**, or a click. **Gamepad**: d-pad and **A**.

## Permissions

- **Storage**: your records. **flagcdn.com**: flag images.
