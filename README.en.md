# Geo

A pixel-art geography quiz: flags, capitals and countries (names in French).

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## French départements (new in 1.2.0)

In the region list, **France (départements)**: the 96 départements of mainland France. Four ways to play: **numbers** (which département is 69?), **prefectures**, **the département of a prefecture**, and the **map** where you click the requested département. The map is hand-drawn: each département takes the land closest to its centre, so the shapes are approximate but the places are right. Paris and its inner ring, too small to click, stay in the three other games.

## Games

- **Flags**: find the country. **Capitals**: find a country’s capital. **Country of a capital**: the other way round.
- **Map**: click the requested country on a pixel world map (zoomed on the chosen continent). Micro-states are left out: too small to click.
- The whole world or one continent; ten questions with four answers.
- Missed countries come back more often; the end of a series lists each mistake with its flag and the right answer.

## Controls

- **1 to 4**, or **arrows** then **Enter**, or a click. **Gamepad**: d-pad and **A**. On the map, a click, or arrows to move the crosshair and **Enter**.
- **Full screen**: the button of the same name; Escape to leave it.

## Permissions

- **Storage**: your records. **flagcdn.com**: flag images.
